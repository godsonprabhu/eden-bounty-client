## 1. Brand Identity & Philosophy

Eiden Bounty is an international wholesale trading and business solutions corporation based in Malaysia. Operating across ten diverse commercial divisions, Eiden Bounty connects critical supply chains, heavy industrial infrastructure, refined energy products, fast-moving commodities, and autonomous agricultural technology between ASEAN and international markets.

### Core Philosophy: The Sovereign Meridian
- **Institutional Gravitas**: Grounded in tangible commerce, maritime logistics, and multilateral trade standards.
- **Architectural Clarity**: Clean grid structures, generous spacing, and unyielding visual hierarchy that convey stability and sovereign-grade reliability.
- **Precision Engineering**: Technical exactness in specifications, metrics, and data presentation, utilizing selective monospaced readouts and laser-focused accents.
- **Factual Integrity**: Zero fabricated metrics, zero artificial hype, and strict compliance regarding corporate affiliations and partner disclosures.

## 4. Brand Voice & Editorial Governance

### Brand Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Authoritative** | Speaks with the calm gravitas of a premier international trading house. Never casual, never desperate. |
| **Institutional** | Respects bilateral trade protocol, international maritime shipping laws, and transparent commercial transactions. |
| **Precise** | Details specific grades, standard tolerances, packaging formats, and logistics hubs rather than vague superlatives. |
| **Industrial** | Grounded in tangible assets, real infrastructure, heavy machinery, and physical bulk commodities. |
| **Technological** | Embraces high-efficiency aerial robotics, automated sorting, and telemetry without falling into sci-fi caricature. |

### Core Attributes

| Attribute | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Sovereign Stability** | Enduring trade relationships anchored in Malaysia's strategic trade corridors. |
| **Wholesale Scale** | Built for institutional volume, containerized bulk freight, and multi-year supply contracts. |
| **Operational Rigor** | Stringent quality testing across all 10 divisions from petroleum distillates to food-grade water. |
| **Transparent Governance** | Open, verifiable trade terms, compliant invoicing, and verified partnership statements. |

### Prohibited

| Avoid | Required Alternative |
|-------|----------------------|
| Generic SaaS marketing jargon ("Disrupting", "Next-gen synergy", "10x solutions") | Concrete commercial terms ("Bilateral wholesale procurement", "Volume distribution") |
| Fabricated statistics or unverified client logos | Verified operational scopes and factual product specifications |
| Claiming official partnership with Garuda Aerospace Limited | Accurate attribution: "Agricultural drone commercial distribution in association with Garuda Aerospace Limited" |
| Low-contrast text or decorative unreadable typography | Strict adherence to WCAG AA (minimum 4.5:1 text contrast) |
| Arbitrary neon gradients and glossy glassmorphism | Solid architectural slate surfaces, crisp 1px borders, and deliberate sovereign gold accents |

## 5. Visual Identity for the 10 Business Divisions

To preserve corporate coherence while giving each sector operational distinction, each division possesses a classified code, a specific accent alignment, and a technical specification framework.

```
DIVISION CODEX ARCHITECTURE:
[EB-DIV-01] MINERAL WATER          → Accent: Azure Blue     (#0284C7) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-02] SCRAP TRADING          → Accent: Steel Slate    (#475569) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-03] CONSTRUCTION           → Accent: Earth Amber    (#D97706) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-04] BUILDING PROMOTIONS    → Accent: Deep Bronze    (#92400E) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-05] SHISHA TOBACCO TRADING → Accent: Tobacco Ocre   (#B45309) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-06] PETROLEUM PRODUCTS     → Accent: Fuel Gold      (#D4AF37) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-07] AXE OIL                → Accent: Camphor Teal   (#0D9488) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-08] INDUSTRIAL LUBRICANTS  → Accent: High-Visc Amber(#EA580C) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-09] FURNACE OIL            → Accent: Thermal Russet (#C2410C) + Sovereign Gold
[EB-DIV-10] AGRICULTURAL DRONES    → Accent: Electric Cyan  (#00E5FF) + Precision Green
```

### Detailed Division Specifications

#### 1. Mineral Water `[EB-DIV-01]`
- **Sector**: Fast-Moving Commercial Commodities & Institutional Bottling.
- **Visual Identity**: Crystal clarity, oceanic off-white backgrounds, pristine filtration imagery.
- **Technical Metadata**: Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) tolerance, pH balance range, packaging formats (350ml, 500ml, 1500ml PET, bulk gallons), international bottling sanitary standards.
- **Key Accent**: Azure Blue `#0284C7` on Maritime Navy `#0A1118`.

#### 2. Scrap Trading `[EB-DIV-02]`
- **Sector**: Heavy Metal Reclamation & Secondary Metallurgy.
- **Visual Identity**: Geometric structural cuts, raw texture of industrial metals, clean processing yards.
- **Technical Metadata**: ISRI Classification (Heavy Melting Steel HMS 1/2, Copper Millberry, Aluminum Extrusions 6063), metric tonnage capacity, radiation-free clearance protocol.
- **Key Accent**: Raw Iron Slate `#475569`.

#### 3. Construction `[EB-DIV-03]`
- **Sector**: Civil Infrastructure & Structural Commercial Development.
- **Visual Identity**: Architectural monolithic silhouettes, structural steel frameworks, cast concrete geometries.
- **Technical Metadata**: ASTM / BS EN steel standard grades, tensile strength ratings, aggregate bulk volume metrics, project site logistics.
- **Key Accent**: Earth Amber `#D97706`.

#### 4. Building Promotions `[EB-DIV-04]`
- **Sector**: Commercial Property Development & Strategic Asset Marketing.
- **Visual Identity**: High-elevation corporate photography, masterplan architectural schematics.
- **Technical Metadata**: Gross Floor Area (GFA), zoning classifications, prime commercial trade hubs in Peninsular Malaysia.
- **Key Accent**: Deep Bronze `#92400E`.

#### 5. Shisha Tobacco Trading `[EB-DIV-05]`
- **Sector**: Regulated Tobacco Distribution & Bulk Shisha Confections.
- **Visual Identity**: Rich warm hues, aromatic leaf textures, sealed compliance-grade packaging.
- **Technical Metadata**: Cut width (mm), moisture content percentage, international customs Harmonized System (HS) codes, excise sealing compliance.
- **Key Accent**: Tobacco Ochre `#B45309`.

#### 6. Petroleum Products `[EB-DIV-06]`
- **Sector**: Hydrocarbon Distillates & Downstream Wholesale Fuel.
- **Visual Identity**: Polished stainless steel pipelines, offshore marine terminals, golden hydrocarbon refraction.
- **Technical Metadata**: Specific Gravity @ 15°C, Flash Point (°C), Sulfur content (ppm/wt%), ASTM D-standard compliance, bulk tanker parcel capacity.
- **Key Accent**: Fuel Gold `#D4AF37`.

#### 7. Axe Oil `[EB-DIV-07]`
- **Sector**: Traditional Medicinal Oils & Consumer Wellness Wholesale.
- **Visual Identity**: Pristine botanical extraction, clinical apothecary packaging, heritage brand integrity.
- **Technical Metadata**: Active camphor/menthol/eucalyptus percentages, tamper-evident carton packaging, multi-carton container shipment manifests.
- **Key Accent**: Camphor Teal `#0D9488`.

#### 8. Industrial Lubricants `[EB-DIV-08]`
- **Sector**: High-Performance Machinery Oils & Heavy Fleet Lubrication.
- **Visual Identity**: Microscopic oil film shear resistance, high-speed turbine shafts, sealed drum arrays.
- **Technical Metadata**: ISO VG Viscosity Grades (32, 46, 68, 100, 220), Kinematic Viscosity @ 40°C & 100°C, Viscosity Index (VI), Four-Ball Wear Scar (mm).
- **Key Accent**: High-Viscosity Amber `#EA580C`.

#### 9. Furnace Oil `[EB-DIV-09]`
- **Sector**: Thermal Industrial Fuel & Marine Bunker Energy.
- **Visual Identity**: High-temperature kiln combustion, industrial boiler infrastructure, maritime bunkering barges.
- **Technical Metadata**: Viscosity CST @ 50°C, Gross Calorific Value (kcal/kg), Pour Point (°C), Ash Content (wt%), RMG/IFO marine grade specs.
- **Key Accent**: Thermal Russet `#C2410C`.

#### 10. Agricultural Drones `[EB-DIV-10]`
- **Sector**: Autonomous Agritech & Precision Aerial Spraying.
- **Commercial Attribution**: Associated with **Garuda Aerospace Limited**; presented strictly within verified commercial distribution channels.
- **Visual Identity**: Ultra-clean aerodynamic carbon fiber, digital telemetry overlays, multispectral crop-health imaging, laser-sharp flight vectors.
- **Technical Metadata**: Payload capacity (liters/hectare), flight endurance under payload, RTK centimeter-precision GPS positioning, spray swath width (meters), spray pump flow rate (L/min).
- **Key Accent**: Electric Cyan `#00E5FF`.

## 6. Layout, Spacing & Architectural Principles

1. **4px / 8px Spatial Grid**:
   All margins, paddings, gaps, and component bounds strictly adhere to an 8px base unit (4px for micro-spacing).
2. **Surface Elevation & Contrast Hierarchy**:
   - Level 0 (Base Canvas): `#0A1118` (Dark) / `#F8FAFC` (Light)
   - Level 1 (Structural Cards): `#1E293B` (Dark) / `#FFFFFF` (Light) with 1px border `#334155` / `#E2E8F0`
   - Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts/Dossiers): `#16202E` (Dark) / `#FFFFFF` (Light) with subtle technical shadow
3. **Border Radii Discipline**:
   Industrial precision requires sharp, controlled edges.
   - Standard cards & containers: `4px` (`rounded-sm`)
   - Buttons, badges, and form controls: `2px` to `4px`
   - No pill-shaped bubbles or oversized floating bubbles (`rounded-3xl` is prohibited).
4. **Motion Discipline**:
   - Animation duration: 200ms - 400ms.
   - Timing curve: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (easeOutExpo).
   - Zero gratuitous bounce, jiggle, or floating loops.
   - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` unconditionally.
